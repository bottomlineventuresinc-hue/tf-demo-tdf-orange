What we will not do

  • Open walls or energize anything before the scope is agreed.
  • Quote a panel upgrade from photos alone.
  • Hide parts costs inside a vague line item.
  • Leave a panel without a clear breaker directory.
  • Ignore a load or safety problem we find while we are there.
